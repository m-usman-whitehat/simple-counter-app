# Fix Dormant Account Without Making App Public

## Understanding the Requirement

Google Play requires you to **"publish an app"** to fix dormant accounts. However, you have options to minimize visibility.

## ✅ Solution: Publish Then Unpublish (Recommended)

### Strategy:
1. **Publish to Production** (required to fix dormant account)
2. **Submit for review** (this counts as "publishing activity")
3. **After approval, unpublish it** (removes from public store)
4. **Account stays active** (publishing activity counts even if later unpublished)

---

## Step-by-Step Plan

### STEP 1: Complete Minimum Requirements (30 minutes)

You still need to complete required sections to submit:

1. **Store Listing** (minimum):
   - App name: "Simple Counter"
   - Short description: "A simple counter app"
   - Full description: (basic description)
   - App icon: Upload 512x512 PNG
   - Screenshots: Upload 2 phone screenshots (minimum)
   - Category: Tools

2. **Policy Declarations** (all 9):
   - Privacy Policy URL (host on Google Sites)
   - All other declarations: No/None

3. **Content Rating**: Complete questionnaire

4. **Pricing**: Set to Free

---

### STEP 2: Publish to Production (5 minutes)

1. Go to **Production** → **Create new release**
2. Upload your AAB
3. Add release notes
4. **Submit for review**

**This counts as "publishing activity" and fixes dormant account!** ✅

---

### STEP 3: After Approval - Unpublish (2 minutes)

Once Google approves your app (1-3 days):

1. Go to **Production** → **Releases**
2. Click on your release
3. Look for **"Unpublish"** or **"Remove from sale"** option
4. Click to unpublish

**Result:**
- ✅ App removed from public store
- ✅ Not visible to users
- ✅ Account stays active (publishing activity already counted)
- ✅ You can republish anytime if needed

---

## Alternative: Keep Published But Unlisted

If "Unpublish" option isn't available:

1. **Keep it published** (it's already submitted)
2. **Don't promote it** (no marketing, no links)
3. **It won't show up prominently** without promotion
4. **Very few people will find it** (unless they search specifically)

**Note:** Even if published, without promotion, it's unlikely to get many downloads.

---

## Why This Works

- ✅ **Publishing to Production** = Counts as "active publishing"
- ✅ **Submitting for review** = Shows account activity
- ✅ **Unpublishing later** = Doesn't reverse the activity credit
- ✅ **Account stays active** = Dormant status removed

- ✅ Production release + Submitted for review = **COUNTS**
- ❌ Internal Testing only = **DOESN'T COUNT**
- ❌ Draft releases = **DOESN'T COUNT**

### Timeline
- **Today:** Complete sections + Submit for review = Account safe

- ✅ Account stays active
