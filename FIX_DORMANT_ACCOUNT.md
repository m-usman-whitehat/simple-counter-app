### STEP 2: Complete Required Sections (30-45 minutes)

Before you can publish to Production, you MUST complete these:

#### A. Store Listing (15 minutes)
1. Go to **Store presence** → **Store listing**
2. Complete:
   - ✅ App name: "Simple Counter"
   - ✅ Short description: "A simple and lightweight counter app for tracking counts and tallies"
   - ✅ Full description: (copy from PUBLISHING_STEPS.md)
   - ✅ App icon: Upload 512x512 PNG (use create-images.html)
   - ✅ Screenshots: Upload at least 2 phone screenshots (required)
   - ✅ Tablet screenshots: Upload at least 1 for 7" and 10" (if marked with *)
   - ✅ Category: Select "Tools"
   - ✅ Tags: counter, tally, counting, utility, tools
3. Click **Save**

#### B. Policy Declarations (15 minutes)
1. Go to **Policy** → **App content**
2. Complete all 9 declarations (see POLICY_DECLARATIONS_GUIDE.md):
   - ✅ Privacy Policy (host on Google Sites - see HOST_PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
   - ✅ App Access: No restrictions
   - ✅ Content Ratings: Complete questionnaire (all "No")
   - ✅ Target Audience: Everyone
   - ✅ Data Safety: No data collection
   - ✅ Advertising ID: No
   - ✅ Government Apps: No
   - ✅ Financial Features: No
   - ✅ Health Apps: No
3. Save each declaration

#### C. Content Rating (5 minutes)
1. In **Policy** → **App content** → **Content ratings**
2. Click **Start questionnaire**
3. Answer all questions (all "No" for your app)
4. Complete and get rating (should be "Everyone")

#### D. Pricing & Distribution (3 minutes)
1. Go to **Pricing & distribution**
2. Select **Free**
3. Select countries (or "All countries")
4. Accept content guidelines
5. Click **Save**

---

### STEP 3: Create Production Release (5 minutes)

1. Go to **Production** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Create new release**
3. Upload your AAB file:
   - If you already have one, use it
   - Or rebuild: Build → Generate Signed Bundle / APK → Android App Bundle
   - Make sure version code is higher than previous (should be 2 or 3)
4. Add release notes:
   ```
   Initial release
   - Simple counter app for tracking counts and tallies
   - Clean and intuitive interface
   - One-tap counting with reset function
   - Lightweight and fast
   ```
5. Click **Save**
6. Click **Review release**
7. Review and click **Start rollout to Production**

---

### STEP 4: Submit for Review (2 minutes) ⚠️ CRITICAL

1. Go to **Production** → **Releases**
2. Check that all required sections show green checkmarks:
   - ✅ Store listing
   - ✅ Content rating
   - ✅ Pricing & distribution
   - ✅ Policy declarations
   - ✅ Release uploaded
3. If all green, click **"Submit for review"** button
4. **DONE!** ✅
